Market Share Dynamics

Google Maps completely dominates "during-trip" utility—people use it incessantly while walking around a new city. However, they almost exclusively use Airbnb, Expedia, TripAdvisor, or Excel spreadsheets for the "pre-trip" planning phase. Google Maps has all the data, but zero robust workflow tools for planning.

💡 The Strategy

The Problem: "The Planning Fragmentation"

Group travel planning is a nightmare. It requires coordinating across WhatsApp chats, shared Google Docs, and dozens of browser tabs. Users constantly cross-reference Airbnbs with Google Maps to check distances, yet Maps itself offers no way to officially bundle places into a cohesive, collaborative itinerary.

Solution Hypothesis: The "Collaborative Planner"

Convert Maps from a single-player utility to a Multiplayer Canvas. By introducing robust, real-time Collaborative Itineraries directly in the 'Saved' tab, groups can vote on places, map out daily clusters, and eventually book directly through Maps seamlessly.

🧠 The Ideation Pool

AI Destination Inspiration

A discovery feed that uses search history and saved places to suggest fully-formed weekend getaway packages, auto-generating a map of points of interest.

2

Collaborative Itinerary Planner

Turn the 'Saved List' into a multiplayer canvas where friends can mutually drag-and-drop locations into "Day 1", "Day 2", and vote on restaurants.

3

Direct In-App Booking System

Aggregating all flight, hotel, and restaurant APIs so the user never leaves the app, culminating in a one-click Google Pay unified checkout.

🎯 Strategic Prioritization

Impact vs. Effort Matrix

Solution 3
Solution 2 (MVP)
Solution 1

Recommendation: Solution 2 (Collaborative Planner)

High Impact, Medium Effort. Chosen as MVP. Maps already has place-saving and primitive list-sharing APIs. Expanding this to real-time sync (multiplayer) is significantly less effort than building a massive Direct Booking payments aggregator, but solves the core pain point immediately.
